# Cadence Tasks Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Cadence Tasks stores routine and task data on your device. If you sign in with
Google, the app syncs that content through Google Firebase services and
associates it with your Firebase user ID.

## Data processed

Google provides your sign-in identity, which can include your user ID, email
address, and display name. The app stores the routine and task titles, notes,
schedules, recurrence settings, completion state, and synchronization metadata
that you create.

## How data is used

Data is used to provide the agenda, routines, reminders, and sync across devices
signed in to the same account. Android reminders are scheduled locally on your
device. Cadence Tasks does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Storage and retention

Local data is stored in the app on your device. Synced data is stored in Cloud
Firestore in the United States multi-region location selected for this project.
It remains there until you delete your account and data.

## Deletion

Use **Settings > Delete account and data** to permanently delete your Cadence
Tasks Firebase account, its synced task and routine documents, and the
corresponding local records on this device. This does not delete your Google
account. A device that is offline during deletion may retain its local copy
until it reconnects and receives the deletion or you clear that device's app
data.

When signed out, use **Settings > Delete data on this device** to remove all
local task and routine data for every account on that device. Google and
Firebase may retain service logs as described in their policies.

## Service providers

Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore process data to
provide sign-in and synchronization. Their services are subject to Google's
privacy terms.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the project issue tracker at
<https://github.com/al7474/pulse-routines/issues>. Do not post task content or
other private information in a public issue.
